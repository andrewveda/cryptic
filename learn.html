THE ART OF CRYPTIC CROSSWORDS

A COMPLETE INITIATION FOR BEGINNERS

1. THE GOLDEN RULE

Every cryptic clue contains two paths to the same answer:

• A definition (straight meaning)
• Wordplay (a coded construction)

These usually sit at the start or end of the clue.

Example:
“Smart girl alternates MBE Vecna zombie (5)”
Definition: Smart girl
Wordplay: Alternating letters → MEENZ

2. INDICATORS: THE SECRET SIGNALS

Cryptic clues announce their tricks openly — but in disguise. These announcing words are called indicators.

Anagram Indicators

Words like: confused, scrambled, wild, broken

Hidden Indicators

Words like: hidden in, part of, inside

Homophone Indicators

Words like: sounds like, reportedly, heard

3. MAJOR CLUE TYPES (WITH LOGIC)

Anagram

Rearrange letters.

“Confused e-name (5)” → scramble → MEENA

Hidden

The answer appears consecutively inside the clue.

Charade

Small pieces placed side by side.

Double Definition

Two straight meanings, same word.

Acrostic

First (or last) letters of consecutive words.

4. GRAMMAR IS LAW

Cryptic clues obey grammar strictly.

• Past indicators give past constructions
• Plurals match plurals
• Articles matter

If the grammar doesn’t fit, the answer is wrong — even if it “feels” right.

5. HOW TO ACTUALLY SOLVE

1. Identify likely definition
2. Spot indicators
3. Count letters (enumeration)
4. Build mechanically
5. Only then admire the surface

6. FINAL INITIATION TRUTH

Cryptic crosswords are not about vocabulary. They are about trusting instructions.

Every clue is honest. Every deception is declared.
